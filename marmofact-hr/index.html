    Board status

    Species

    Sets the board's detector and this page's: marmoset 150-480 bpm on the green channel, human 30-220 bpm on IR. Automatic detection reads the pulse rate: under 150 bpm is a human, over 220 a marmoset, and it switches after a few seconds of agreement. In between (a running human, a very calm marmoset) it leaves the choice alone.

    LEDs

    Codes 0-255, 0.2 mA each. The board boots at 50 (10 mA). On the tail the pulse was at the noise floor at 10 mA; 150,60,150 is the PC default to try. Keep IRDC under about 400,000 and OPT=ok.

    Session

    The pre-roll is the status-only pause hostlib/receiver.py also makes: the board sends its constant fields (BOARD, IMU, CHARGE) only while idle.

    About

    Talks to HealthMonitor1 (CH591D firmware v6/v7) over Web Bluetooth, with the same protocol and detector as hostlib/ and the iPhone app. Works in Chrome or Edge on a Mac, Windows, Linux or Android. Safari and Firefox have no Web Bluetooth. Keep this tab in front while recording: a hidden or sleeping tab stops receiving.
