This is the custom hardware behind our functional ultrasound imaging in awake marmosets: a head implant that holds the head steady over a cranial window, and a chair that seats the animal under the probe. Each model below is the actual CAD design. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and click a model to take it apart or put it back together. The original CAD files can be downloaded with an access code.

Head implant

The implant fixes the head during imaging. The ultrasound probe looks into the brain through a cranial window in the skull.

Click the model to show its parts

Implant on the skull

  • Marmoset skull
  • Frame with headpost
  • Cover plate

The implant in place on a marmoset skull model. The curved underside of the frame follows the top of the skull, with the headpost pointing toward the back of the head. The frame carries the headpost, which is what the chair holds to keep the head still. The cover plate sits on top of the frame and fastens through three screw holes.

Implant size: 40.0 × 20.9 × 14.9 mm
