On August 14, 2026 I hosted an **Emerging Neurotech Seminar** at MIT on the theme of *Ultrasound in Neuroscience*. Two speakers joined us to talk about where the technique is heading — one on imaging through the intact skull, one on wearable ultrasound outside the brain entirely.

### Speakers

**Yaoheng (Mack) Yang** — Assistant Professor, Department of Biomedical Engineering, University of Southern California
*Acoustic transparency enabling functional ultrasound imaging through mouse and human skulls*

**Dian Li** — Chee C. Tung (1966) PhD Fellow, Department of Mechanical Engineering, MIT
*Dexterous hand tracking using wearable wrist ultrasonic imaging*

### Details

- **When:** Friday, August 14, 2026, 12:00 – 1:00 PM
- **Where:** Building 46-3189, MIT
- **Host:** Chaoyi Zhang, Postdoctoral Fellow, Guoping Feng Lab

Supported by the SQoL Program, MIT School of Science, and the Feng Lab at the McGovern Institute. Thanks to everyone who came out — and to both speakers for two genuinely complementary talks.
