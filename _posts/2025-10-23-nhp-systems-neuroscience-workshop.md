I attended the [10 Questions for NHP Systems Neuroscience Workshop 2025](https://www.cnbc.cmu.edu/10-questions-for-nhp-systems-neuroscience-workshop-2025/), held October 23–25, 2025 at the historic Mellon Institute at Carnegie Mellon University in Pittsburgh, PA.

A lot of science, a lot of fun, and a lot of friends.

One highlight was a session where each participant posed two guiding questions for the field:
