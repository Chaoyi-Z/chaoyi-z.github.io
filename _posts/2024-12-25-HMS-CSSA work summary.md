It's been two years since I joined HMS-CSSA in October 2022, my arrival in Boston. My journey has taken me from a regular member to a committee member, where I've been fortunate to contribute to numerous activities, from planning to successful execution. This experience has been a mix of challenges and immense satisfaction.

As I step down from my role, I'm eager to dedicate more time to my scientific pursuits. I am deeply grateful to the HMS-CSSA community for helping me adjust to life in Boston. I cherish the friendships I've made and the valuable experience I've gained in organizing and hosting scientific seminars.
