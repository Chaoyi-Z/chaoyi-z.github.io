As part of the Cambridge Science Festival, I joined "Decoding the Brain" at the MIT Museum, where neuroscientists from MIT's McGovern Institute shared their latest discoveries in brain science. Along with Henry Hall and Lace Riggs, I ran an interactive lab demo letting visitors compare real human, sheep, and mouse brains.
