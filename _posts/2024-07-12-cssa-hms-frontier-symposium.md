**Sai Zhou** — Ph.D. Candidate, Material Science and Engineering Program, University of California, San Diego

**Chonghe Wang, Ph.D.** — Co-founder and CTO, Sonologi, Inc. (MIT)

Forbes 30 Under 30 (North America) · Finalist, National Inventors Hall of Fame · 2023 Highly Cited Researcher (Clarivate)

### Details

- **When:** Friday, July 12, 2024, 9:00 – 10:00 PM EDT
- **Where:** Zoom
- **Organized by:** HMS-CSSA (Chinese Scientists and Scholars Association at Harvard Medical School)

Great to see so much interest in wearable ultrasound technology — thanks to both speakers for sharing their work, and to everyone who joined the call.
